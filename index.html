Privacy Policy for “Minimalist To-Do”

Effective date: October 12, 2026

In one sentence: this app does not collect any personal data from you.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs.

2. Where your data lives

  • Tasks and groups are stored on your device only by default.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your data is stored in your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot access it.

3. Notifications & Siri

  • Reminders use local iOS notifications and only fire on your device, with your permission.
  • Adding or completing tasks via Siri / Shortcuts is handled entirely on your device.

4. Tips (In-App Purchase)

Optional tips are handled by Apple’s In-App Purchase. All payment information is processed by Apple; the developer never has access to it.
